04 / PRODUCT CASE STUDY

AI-powered e-commerce discovery

Smart Product Discovery

Problem: Traditional filters can struggle with natural-language needs such as “a lightweight laptop for coding and travel under my budget.”

User story: As a shopper, I want to describe what I need in natural language so I can quickly discover relevant products.

AI solution: Interpret intent, extract constraints, map them to product attributes, retrieve relevant catalog items and explain matches.

MVP: Prompt → intent extraction → catalog retrieval → ranked results → explanation → refinement.

Success metrics: Search-to-product-click rate, add-to-cart rate, time to relevant result, refinement rate and relevance feedback.

Risks: Hallucinated attributes, stale catalog data, biased recommendations and unclear explanations; use grounded catalog data and transparent attributes.

Concept case study — not a claim of a deployed feature.
