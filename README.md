## 📊 How I Document AI Products

For each major project, I aim to document:

1. **Problem** — What user/business problem exists?
2. **Users** — Who experiences the problem?
3. **Opportunity** — Why solve it now?
4. **Solution** — What product experience addresses it?
5. **AI Role** — Where does AI add value?
6. **PRD** — Requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria
7. **Metrics** — Activation, adoption, quality, retention, efficiency or business KPIs
8. **Trade-offs** — Cost, latency, accuracy, privacy, complexity and usability
9. **Experimentation** — How the product idea would be validated
10. **Learnings** — What changed after testing?
